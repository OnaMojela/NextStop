# Notes for judges

NextStop turns the 27-hour Pretoria–Cape Town rail journey into ten places worth getting
off at. Press **Ride the line** and the train runs the route on its own, standing at each
platform while that stop's attractions, story and local food open beside the map — or drag
the kilometre spine, where every station sits at its true distance from Pretoria, so the
six empty hours of Karoo between Kimberley and De Aar look like six hours. Search reads
all of it rather than just station names: `brandy` returns Worcester, `diamond` returns
Kimberley. The route and the 1 440 km are computed from 47 points plotted along the real
line, and the ten stops, thirty attractions and ten stories were researched and written by
us. The data layer is shaped for the Supabase tables but reads from a seeded file — we had
no credentials during the build and chose not to fake a connection.
